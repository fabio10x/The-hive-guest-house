About The Hive Guest House

Discover our story, our mission, and the values that make us a preferred choice for travelers.

Our Story

Founded with a passion for genuine hospitality, The Hive Guest House was established to provide a welcoming and comfortable haven for travelers in Addis Ababa. We believe that a guest house should be more than just a place to sleep; it should be a place where guests feel valued, cared for, and completely at home.

From our carefully designed rooms to our attentive staff, every detail is crafted to enhance your experience. Our name, "The Hive," reflects our commitment to creating a vibrant yet peaceful community for our guests, buzzing with positive energy and warmth.

Our Mission

To provide an exceptional and personalized lodging experience by offering immaculate, comfortable accommodations and outstanding customer service that creates a welcoming and memorable stay for every guest.

Our Vision

To be recognized as the leading guest house in Addis Ababa, known for our commitment to quality, community, and creating a true "home away from home" atmosphere that brings guests back time and again.

Explore Kirkos, Addis Ababa

The Hive is located in the vibrant Kirkos neighborhood, a government hub with easy access to Meskel Square, historical museums, upscale restaurants, and major hotels. Everything you need to explore Addis Ababa is right at your doorstep.
